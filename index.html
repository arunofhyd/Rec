Architectural Principles

Built without compromises.

Designed for creators, engineers, and power users who demand uncompromising fidelity without bloat.

01 / ENGINE SCK NATIVE

Apple ScreenCaptureKit

Zero third-party audio drivers. No BlackHole, Soundflower, or dangerous kernel extensions. Built on Apple's modern ScreenCaptureKit APIs for hardware-accelerated, zero-copy video processing.

Direct GPU Pipeline · Metal Accelerated
02 / AUDIO INTERNAL + MIC

Simultaneous System Sound

Record your Zoom calls, YouTube references, Spotify playback, DAW sessions, and microphone at the same time in sample-accurate synchronization.

32-bit Float PCM · Zero Latency Drift
03 / HUD LIVE ANNOTATIONS

Laser Writer & Vector Shapes

Draw live during recording with glowing, auto-fading laser trails, pens, highlighters, arrows, and boxes. The annotation HUD is automatically excluded from the final recording.

Smart Window Exclusion · Floating Canvas
04 / STUDIO MULTI-BLOCK CUT

In-App Multi-Cut Trimmer

Cut out coughs, awkward silences, or mistakes directly inside the app without opening Premiere or QuickTime. Mark multiple cut regions, preview jump transitions, and save in-place.

Lossless Stitching · In-Place Trim
05 / PRIVACY AIR-GAPPED

Your Screen, Your Machine

Rec never sends a single byte to the cloud. No analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no accounts. Confidential code, internal meetings, and personal screens stay strictly on your Mac.

Zero Outbound Traffic · Sandboxed
06 / ARCHITECTURE UNIVERSAL BINARY

Pure Swift & AppKit

Fat binary compiled for both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs. Launches in under 50 milliseconds and sits invisibly in your menu bar using less than 20MB of memory.

Sub-50ms Launch · Fat Mach-O Binary
